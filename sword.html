BLOOD & STEEL

A duel to the death in the pit
Desktop — click to grab the mouse. WASD move, Shift sprint, Space dodge.
Left click strikes in the direction you last moved the mouse: flick left or right to slash, down to chop, up to thrust. Q chop, E thrust.
Right click (hold) blocks. Block just before a blow lands to parry and stagger your foe. Tab toggles lock-on.
G toggles god mode (also on this menu) for testing. C kicks through a guard. X hurls a fireball at whoever is nearest the centre of the screen (marked with a glowing diamond) and sets them alight. F executes a staggered or maimed foe. R takes up a fallen weapon.
Touch — left thumb moves. Swipe the right side to strike in that direction, tap for a quick slash. Hold BLOCK, tap DODGE, KICK or FIRE; EXECUTE and TAKE appear when you can use them.
Heavy blows can take limbs. A killing blow to the head takes the head. Staggered and crippled foes cannot defend themselves.
