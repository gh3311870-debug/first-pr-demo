FRONTLINEDUST VEIL

0600 hrs. Insurgents have taken the village of Qal'at Rahim. Lead your four-man squad in through the south checkpoint and clear every hostile from the compound. Snipers hold the rooftops and towers; shotgunners wait indoors. Carry an M4, a scoped DMR, a pump shotgun and a pistol, and grab spare ammo from fallen enemies.
